## QA scenario owner

`tools/explain-review.ts` — R: retain the served collection owner: 140 catalog cases, actual page parse, caption/artwork, mobile+desktop overflow, no external connections, independent response-header recipe checks, two embeds and site-export card checks. It samples the catalog-selected step, not every step or action. Default screenshots are evidence only; no screenshot oracle compares them. Its protocol and authored-model assertions overlap cheaper suites but its full built-collection delivery scope is distinct. It is not a substitute for the state-machine browser owner or a CPU/latency benchmark.

## Review limitations

No blanket deletion based on duration or static data. Read-only audit ran no broad test suite and claims no new timing measurements. The historical baseline run is reused only after exact tree equality. Adversarial implementation-only review and mutation proof are required before implementation is called complete.
