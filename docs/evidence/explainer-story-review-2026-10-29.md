# Explainer entry and story review — 29 October 2026

Scope: all 27 catalog entries and their declared default settings, plus alternate-case challenges for the changed scenes. The writing review followed [`anti-slop-writing`](https://github.com/adewale/anti-slop-writing): keep source-backed specifics, name the question before the machinery, and connect each step to the next rather than adding generic adjectives. This is an editorial and navigation review, **not** independent verification of every technical claim in 142 cases.

## Adversarial findings and resolutions

- All 27 Rayner gallery tiles linked to a curated *poster step*, not the lesson's beginning (23 middle, four final). `EXPLAINERS[].step` still selects the poster, but `galleryTile()` now links to `<id>.html`. The two embed-lab demos also open at their first step. Individual step share pages and deliberate observation permalinks are untouched. The website's 27 branded tiles and cache-request CTA now open at the beginning; its separate image link to `mr-cache?step=after` remains a deliberate observation link.
- An earlier draft audit incorrectly treated the first enumerated case as the default. **Retracted:** the supposed headline/default mismatches for `cert-renewal`, `quotient-familial`, `ext4-space`, `trust-proxy`, `tailwind-gamut`, `wcag-map` and `isdigit`. The setting declarations, not case order, select the default: respectively 45d/fixed30, one child/65,673.88 €, 1 TiB/-m 5, two hops/attacker skipping CDN, shade 500/sRGB, AA/2.4, and superscript two/isdigit. Their existing conclusions actually support their titles. No numerical outcome or title was changed on the strength of that mistaken inference.
- The first step of `2password`, `gtfs`, `lantern`, `fork`, `glyph`, `agentic-mermaid` and `pen-and-pencil` named an implementation before giving the reader a reason to follow it. Openings now ask what the code or data must resolve, while retaining the named source and concrete evidence. `pen-and-pencil` explicitly identifies its diagram as a model of the pele page's method, not the page's code; its title describes the fixed-box result the scene actually draws. The author's intended emphasis for this title remains a review question, not a new claim about that source.
- Other source-backed opening transitions now lead from the puzzle to the calculation or comparison in `cert-renewal`, `quotient-familial`, `ext4-space`, `trust-proxy`, `tailwind-gamut`, `wcag-map` and `isdigit`. The Certbot limit ending now explains why the rate-limit aside matters after counting renewals. No statistics, Unicode counts, dates, rates or standards claims were invented.
- **Attacks against the revisions:** the zero-child tax case cannot say an *existing child* saves tax, so its introduction now branches on `enfants`; the Display P3 case cannot promise to show only an sRGB screen, so its introduction refers to the selected screen. The already-labeled Mermaid case has no repair step: its first-step text says so, and the ending now calls its commit an agent edit rather than always a typed repair. The GTFS alternative with departures must not be told that it has none: its opening says a route row alone does not promise a departure. The service-account and desktop-approval endings of `2password`, and the second drawing in `pen-and-pencil`, were inspected without changing their conditional outcomes.
