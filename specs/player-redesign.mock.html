Rayner player redesign

A mock of the proposed stepper, built with the player's own tokens and the KV-cache explainer's real words. The desktop frame is 1180 px wide in an 800 px tall window; the phone frame is 390 px, shown in the dark theme. Tags name the requirements in specs/player-redesign.md.

The metaphor is a narrated slide build: the author orders the steps, the reader sets the pace, and every step rests on a picture with its caption. The step bar borrows the stories pattern for position: one segment per step, the current one filling as it plays, a tap to jump. The video player’s vocabulary goes: no Replay, no Stop, no “Fit stage”.

Pictures are sketches of the step 3 resting picture, not renders. Nothing here is interactive.

Steps

Transcript
  1. R1One card: picture, stepper row and words share a surface and a border. The two Next buttons become one.
  2. R2The stage is capped so picture, stepper row and the first caption line fit an 800 px window. The stepper row sits straight under the picture, so it is on screen at load. Prev and Next stay together at the start of the row, where the current player has them; the step bar follows, so Next never drifts with the width.
  3. R3The step bar: one segment per step, done segments in the accent, the current one taller and filled by the step's animation, later ones quiet. Each is a button that jumps to its step. Position and extent are visible while reading.
  4. R4"Step 3 of 4", the title and the line show on every width, not only on phones.
  5. R5The rail sits beside the card when the page is at least 1100 px wide. Each item shows the step's title and its line. The current step is marked by colour, not weight, so nothing shifts.
  6. R6View tools stand apart from step navigation behind a rule: zoom, Whole picture while zoomed, copy link, enlarge. Replay is gone; Prev then Next plays a step again, and a tap on the current segment while it plays rests the picture at once.
